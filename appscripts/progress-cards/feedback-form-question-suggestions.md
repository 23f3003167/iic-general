# Training Feedback Form: Suggested Questions

These are suggestions only. Add them manually to the existing Google Form. The Apps Script does not edit the form.

## Recommended Scale

For each activity, consider adding a required 1–5 linear scale. Put the activity-specific prompt in the question description/help text:

- 1 = Not helpful
- 2 = Slightly helpful
- 3 = Moderately helpful
- 4 = Very helpful
- 5 = Extremely helpful

Follow each scale with an optional paragraph question. Use this description: **What worked well, and what is one change that would improve this activity?** Keeping comments optional should make the form easier to complete while still allowing detailed feedback. These form responses are for program evaluation and are not displayed in the progress card.

## Activity Ratings

1. **Pre-Placement Module (PPM)**
	- Suggested scale title: `How would you rate the Pre-Placement Module (PPM)?`
	- Prompt: How helpful was PPM in preparing you for professional communication and workplace readiness?
2. **Self Introduction**
	- Suggested scale title: `How would you rate Self Introduction?`
	- Prompt: How helpful was this activity in helping you introduce yourself clearly and confidently?
3. **Listening and Speaking (LS)**
	- Suggested scale title: `How would you rate Listening and Speaking (LS)?`
	- Prompt: How helpful was LS in improving your listening, comprehension, and spoken responses?
4. **Listening and Writing (LW)**
	- Suggested scale title: `How would you rate Listening and Writing (LW)?`
	- Prompt: How helpful was LW in improving your written responses and understanding of written communication?
5. **Email Writing**
	- Suggested scale title: `How would you rate Email Writing?`
	- Prompt: How helpful was this activity in preparing you to write clear, professional emails?
6. **Self Aptitude**
	- Suggested scale title: `How would you rate Self Aptitude?`
	- Prompt: How helpful was this activity in assessing and strengthening your aptitude skills?
7. **Behavioral Assessment (BA)**
	- Suggested scale title: `How would you rate the Behavioral Assessment (BA)?`
	- Prompt: How helpful was BA in preparing you to explain your experiences, decisions, and work approach?
8. **Presentation**
	- Suggested scale title: `How would you rate Presentation?`
	- Prompt: How helpful was this activity in improving your organization, delivery, and confidence?
9. **Tech MCQ**
	- Suggested scale title: `How would you rate Tech MCQ?`
	- Prompt: How helpful was Tech MCQ in assessing and strengthening your technical knowledge?
10. **AI Mock Interview**
	- Suggested scale title: `How would you rate the AI Mock Interview?`
	 - Prompt: How helpful was the AI mock interview in preparing you for interview questions and responses?
11. **1-on-1 with Course Instructor**
	- Suggested scale title: `How would you rate the 1-on-1 with the Course Instructor?`
	 - Prompt: How helpful was the instructor session in giving you personalized guidance and next steps?
12. **Activity Points**
	- Suggested scale title: `How would you rate the Activity Points requirements?`
	 - Prompt: How clear and achievable were the activity-point requirements?

For every comment item, use the optional prompt: **What worked well, and what is one change that would improve this activity?**

## Overall Questions

Keep the existing overall feedback question. Consider adding these optional prompts if they are not already covered:

- Which training activity helped you the most, and why?
- What is the most important improvement you would recommend for the overall training?
- Is there anything else you would like the IIC Training Team to know?

## Form Clarity

- Explain that ratings refer to the student's own experience and that comments are optional.
- Keep the existing video-publication consent question separate from completion eligibility. State clearly whether choosing “No” affects training completion; it should not be ambiguous.
- Keep student email required so the submitted feedback can be matched to the progress card.
- Since CSM is composed of Self Introduction, LS, LW, and Email Writing, the four activity ratings above provide specific CSM feedback without requiring a redundant CSM rating.
